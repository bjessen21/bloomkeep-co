Your Bouquet, Your Colors

A Bouquet Built to Last Every Photo

Your bridal bouquet is in more photos than almost anything else on your wedding day — the first look, the ceremony, the bouquet toss, portraits an hour after your hair and makeup are done. A fresh bouquet is already wilting by the time you're through cocktail hour; a faux bouquet from Flower Rental OKC looks exactly the same in your last photo as it did in your first.

We design bridal and bridesmaid bouquets from real-touch silk and faux stems, hand-shaded and finished the same way a fresh florist would build them — not pulled from a plastic multi-pack. Boutonnières and corsages for your wedding party are built to match, so everyone's flowers coordinate without a single stem wilting between the ceremony and the reception.

  • Bridal bouquet designed to your exact color palette and style
  • Matching bridesmaid bouquets, any quantity
  • Boutonnières and corsages for your full wedding party
  • Real-touch materials that photograph like fresh flowers
  • Available to rent-and-return, or purchase to keep as a keepsake
  • Free design consultation before you commit to anything
