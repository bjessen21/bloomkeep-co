Centerpieces That Look the Same at 9 PM as They Did at 5

Reception centerpieces sit out for hours — through dinner, toasts, and dancing — and fresh arrangements start showing it well before the night is over. Common Questions

Centerpiece Rental FAQ

Do you set up the day of the wedding, or the day before?

Timing depends on your venue's access windows — most setups happen the morning of or the afternoon before, coordinated directly with your venue coordinator.

Can centerpieces be different at each table?

Yes — many couples mix in a few statement pieces at head tables with simpler arrangements elsewhere. We'll plan that mix during your consultation.

What happens if a centerpiece is damaged during the reception?

Normal wear from a full evening of use is expected and built into our pricing. Significant damage beyond normal use may incur a replacement fee, which we'll go over before you book.
