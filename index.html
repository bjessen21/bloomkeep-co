Why Couples Choose Faux

The Same Romantic Look, None of the Wedding-Day Risk

Fresh flowers are beautiful for about six hours — long enough for photos, not long enough to survive an Oklahoma afternoon of wind, heat, or a surprise spring storm. Couples across the metro are discovering that today's faux and silk florals hold their color and shape from the morning of setup through the very last reception photo, with zero risk of a wilted bouquet or a centerpiece that browns before dinner is served.

It's also a budget story. A full fresh floral package for an Oklahoma City-area wedding commonly runs well into the thousands once you add ceremony arrangements, a bridal party's worth of bouquets, and reception centerpieces for every table. Renting faux arrangements gets you the same visual impact — often designed by hand from the same premium suppliers florists use — for a fraction of the cost, freeing up budget for the venue, the band, or the honeymoon.

  • No wilting, browning, or bruised petals in transit or setup
  • Designed and finished by hand, not mass-produced from a factory box
  • Rent once, walk away — no vases or greenery to return to a florist the next morning
  • A sustainable alternative that doesn't end up in a dumpster the day after your wedding

Common Questions

Wedding Flower Rental FAQ

Do faux and silk wedding flowers actually look real?

Yes — the materials used in today's high-end silk and faux floral design have improved dramatically from craft-store artificial flowers. Real-touch petals, hand-shaded coloring, and weighted stems mean our arrangements photograph and hold up under close inspection the way fresh flowers do, without wilting in Oklahoma's summer heat or wind.

How much can we save renting flowers instead of using fresh?

Most couples save 40-60% compared to a full fresh floral package. A complete fresh wedding florals package in the OKC metro commonly runs $2,000-$4,000+, while a comparable faux rental package typically runs $800-$2,200 depending on guest count.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend 4-9 months ahead, especially for dates around OU home game weekends and graduation, when venues book up fastest. We can often still accommodate requests inside 6-8 weeks — ask and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Can we customize colors and style?

Yes. As a local, full-service studio rather than a ship-to-you catalog, we design around your actual colors, venue, and vision rather than only offering fixed collections.

Do we get to keep any of the flowers?

Most packages are rental-and-return, which keeps pricing well below fresh. A purchase option is available on request if you'd like to keep your bouquet or full suite as a keepsake.

Do you handle delivery, setup, and pickup?

Yes. Full-service packages include delivery, on-site setup, and post-event pickup. A self-pickup option is also available for couples who want to save on the delivery fee.
